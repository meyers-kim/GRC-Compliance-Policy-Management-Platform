Information Security Policy

Version: 1.2

This example document defines baseline security principles, access responsibilities, and acceptable use expectations for employees.

Purpose

To protect company information assets and support a consistent security posture.

Scope

Applies to employees, contractors, systems, and company-managed devices.

Key Rules

  • Use strong authentication and protect credentials.
  • Report incidents through the official escalation process.
  • Follow least-privilege access principles.
